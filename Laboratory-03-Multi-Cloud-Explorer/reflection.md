# Mission Reflection

This laboratory activity helped me understand the differences and similarities between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud because of its strong capabilities in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. These technologies are important for modern cloud applications and can support organizations with advanced computing requirements.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because all three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, security, identity management, and application development services. They also provide scalable infrastructure that allows organizations to adjust their resources based on their needs.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection because organizations have different technologies, budgets, workloads, and goals. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence may prefer Google Cloud.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider should be based on the client's actual requirements rather than popularity. Factors such as existing technology, scalability, availability, performance, and future growth should be considered before making a decision.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because I added a structured laboratory folder containing research documents, comparison tables, client recommendations, a decision matrix, Linux investigation results, and screenshots. This makes my portfolio more organized and provides evidence of my cloud computing activities.
